ABOUT

Engineering across hardware, software and AI.

My work has evolved from Mechatronics Engineering into software and AI systems engineering, while retaining the systems thinking, control discipline and physical-world perspective that shaped my engineering foundation.

BACKGROUND

From mechatronics to systems engineering

I studied Mechatronics, Robotics and Automation Engineering at Dedan Kimathi University of Technology. That training combined mechanical systems, electronics, automation, control and computing.

I now apply the same engineering approach to software: decompose complex problems, define interfaces and constraints, implement deliberately, test behavior, measure outcomes and verify claims.

CURRENT DIRECTION

Software & AI Systems Engineering

My current focus is building intelligent systems with clear architectures and observable behavior. This includes full-stack applications, autonomous software, AI integration, distributed-system concepts, experimentation and verification.

Tiannara is the primary private system where these ideas converge. ASC and EFDI are research tracks within that system, not separate products or repositories.
